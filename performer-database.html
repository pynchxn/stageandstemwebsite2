Performer Database

We're building a database of brilliant performers to fill the calendar at Stage & Stem — musicians, comedians, theatre-makers, poets, dancers, cabaret artists and everything in between. If that sounds like you, add your details and we'll be in touch when something fits. Many of these are paid opportunities.

Before you start

  • Your act name and a short description of what you do
  • A link to a show reel or video
  • Instagram, TikTok and Facebook handles (if you have them)
  • Whether you're Cardiff-based
  • Whether you have your own equipment
