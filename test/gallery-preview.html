Eyeball harness for gallery.js — the click-to-enlarge lightbox used on about.html. Open it through a local static server run from the project root (so ../Photos/ resolves), e.g. python3 -m http.server then /test/gallery-preview.html. Click a thumbnail; page with the arrows, the ←/→ keys, or a swipe; close with Esc, the ×, or a click on the backdrop. Not uploaded to Fasthosts.
